# Route Verification & Boarding Realities
## Ground-Truth Verification for Student Corridors

This document details the ground-truth verification of student transit routes in BusBridge, specifically addressing suburban walking realities, boarding stops, and the flagship **CIT Kundrathur ➔ Kilambakkam (KCBT)** transit corridor.

---

## 1. Ground Truth: CIT Kundrathur Campus Access

### Physical Location
- **Institution:** Chennai Institute of Technology (CIT)
- **Address:** Sarathy Nagar, Nandhambakkam Post, Kundrathur, Chennai - 600069.
- **Coordinates:** `12.9716° N, 80.0412° E`
- **Road Frontage:** Situated off the Somangalam–Kundrathur link road.

### Crucial Boarding Fact
> **MTC municipal route buses (such as Route 66K) do NOT drive into or stop directly at the private CIT campus gate.**

Claiming that municipal transit buses stop inside private campus grounds misleads freshers and students. Instead, BusBridge models the verified ground reality:
1. **Puthuper Bus Stop (350m):**
   - 4-minute walk from the CIT hostel/main gate.
   - Frequent shared feeder autos and local mini-buses pick up students here.
   - Fare to Kundrathur Bus Stand: ₹15 per student.
2. **Kundrathur Bus Stand (6.2 km):**
   - The official municipal terminus where Route 66K originates from Platform 1.
   - Boarding at Kundrathur Bus Stand guarantees students seating space and luggage accommodation before the bus enters crowded suburban stops.

## 2. Route 66K Verification Profile

- **Official Service Code:** MTC Route 66K
- **Origin:** Kundrathur Bus Stand
- **Destination:** Kilambakkam Kalaignar Centenary Bus Terminus (KCBT)
- **Distance:** ~28.5 km
- **Average Run Time:** 65 to 80 minutes (depending on GST Road traffic)
- **Fleet:** Standard Ordinary (white board / fare concessions) and Deluxe (MTC green/white liveries)
- **Operating Fleet Size:** High frequency during morning (06:30 – 09:30) and evening peak hours (16:00 – 20:30).

### Official Stop Sequence Verified
1. **Kundrathur Bus Stand (Terminus Platform 1)**
2. Kundrathur Murugan Temple Arch
3. Anakaputhur Amman Koil
4. Pammal Main Road
5. Pallavaram Railway Station & GST Road Junction
6. Chromepet Bus Stand (MIT Gate)

10. Perungalathur (Suburban Bus Bay)
11. Vandalur Zoo (Arignar Anna Zoological Park)
12. Kolapakkam / Urapakkam
13. **Kilambakkam KCBT (Arrival Platform 10)**

## 3. Transfer Verification at Tambaram West

When Route 66K has a dispatch gap, the verified alternate corridor is the **Tambaram West Interchange**:
1. Students take Route 566 or a direct shared auto from Kundrathur to Tambaram West Bus Stand (20–25 mins).
2. At Tambaram, an MTC bus heading south along GST Road to Kilambakkam departs **every 3 to 5 minutes** (Routes 70, G18, E18, 91R, 170A).
3. Total combined transit time is approximately 65–75 minutes, offering an alternative for Friday evening rushes.

## 4. Kilambakkam KCBT Arrival & Concourse Verification

- **MTC City Bus Platform:** Buses terminate at Platforms 5 through 12, located directly facing the GST Road entrance concourse.
- **Pedestrian Connectivity:** A covered, all-weather canopy connects the MTC platform directly to the main terminal building.
- **Intercity Transit Zones:**
  - **SETC (Deep South & Interstate):** Located in the Eastern finger bays.
  - **TNSTC Villupuram / Kumbakonam (Central & Delta):** Located in Central bays.
  - **TNSTC Salem / Coimbatore (Western Corridor):** Located in Western bays.
- **Ticketing & Enquiry:** Official enquiry counter situated at the main rotunda with 24x7 staff and toll-free helpline **149**.
